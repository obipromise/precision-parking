How to play

Goal: stop the car fully inside the glowing bay, lined up with the arrow, then hold still for a second or shift to P.

Phone: turn the steering wheel with your thumb, press GAS and BRAKE, and tap P, R or D to change gear. The wheel re-centers by itself as you roll, like a real car.

Keyboard: ↑ drives forward, ↓ reverses (the gear switches automatically when you stop), ← → steer, Space brakes, P parks, C changes the camera.

Scoring: 300 for parking, up to 500 for accuracy (angle and centering), up to 400 for speed. Every collision costs 100, every cone 40. Too much damage wrecks the car.

Tips: the yellow guide lines show where your bumper corners will go. The beeper speeds up as you get close to something. Real parking is slow. Rushing costs more than it saves.
